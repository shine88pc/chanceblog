On October 26, 2026, the European Climate, Infrastructure and Environment Executive Agency (CINEA) [announced](https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/cef-energy-2026-call-pcipmi-projects-attracts-strong-interest-67-applications-requesting-eur288-2026-10-26_en) that the 2026 call under the Connecting Europe Facility (CEF) Energy programme had received 67 applications for cross-border energy infrastructure projects. Together they ask for €2.88 billion in EU co-funding, and the proposals cover more than 20 countries. The call closed on September 30, 2026.

The budget available for the 2026 calls is €600 million, so the requests add up to nearly five times that amount (€2.88 billion ÷ €600 million = 4.8, our calculation). These are requests for funding, and the announcement reports no award.

### Who the call is for

The call is open to projects that the EU has designated Projects of Common Interest (PCIs) or Projects of Mutual Interest (PMIs). CINEA describes PCIs as key infrastructure projects for completing the EU's internal energy market and reaching its energy and climate goals, such as a climate-neutral economy by 2050. PMIs are key cross-border energy projects linking the EU with neighbouring countries outside it. Under the TEN-E Regulation, projects with either status benefit from faster permit-granting and improved regulatory treatment, and they can apply for CEF Energy funding.

### Where the process stands

When the announcement was published, the applications were being checked against the admissibility and eligibility requirements. Those that qualify will be assessed by independent external experts, the European Commission and CINEA. The announcement does not list the criteria they will use. Applicants are due to be told the results in January 2027, and grant agreements with the selected projects are expected to be signed by July 2027.

Our reading, not CINEA's: with requests at 4.8 times the budget, the call cannot fund everything that was asked for unless more money is added.

### The record since 2014

CINEA says CEF Energy has supported 221 PCI and PMI actions since 2014, with €8.87 billion in EU funding, and that this support contributed to 168 PCI and PMI projects.

### What the announcement leaves out

The announcement does not split the 67 applications between PCIs and PMIs or by technology, sector or country, apart from saying that the proposals cover more than 20 countries. An image on the page, titled “Submission results of the 2026 PCI & PMI calls”, may show more detail, but its contents are not given in the text and this article does not describe them.
